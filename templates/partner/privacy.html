🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Information we collect

Sivdhi may process information supplied during Partner registration and account use, including name, mobile number, email, company or partner profile details, address, bank details, referral information, assigned-worker details and account activity.

How information is used

Information is used to operate your Partner account, verify account and bank details, coordinate permitted partner functions, provide booking and worker status information, record eligible wallet settlements, send relevant notifications and respond to support requests.

Booking, worker and location information

Booking and service-location details may be processed to coordinate service delivery and identify eligible workers. The assigned Worker's profile photo, name, public Worker ID and service skills may be shown to the customer for that booking through My Bookings and the platform-issued Sivdhi Worker ID Card. This card is used for platform identity display and is not a government identity document or proof of government/police verification. Access to customer and worker information is limited to authorised purposes connected with the booking and platform operation.

Bank and payment information

Bank details, wallet records and transaction references may be used for verification, payout processing and financial reconciliation. Demo or sandbox payment records are not real collected funds. Do not send passwords, OTPs, PINs or full payment credentials in ordinary support messages.

Information sharing

Information may be processed by authorised Sivdhi personnel, workers involved in a booking, payment or messaging providers, hosting providers, or lawful authorities where required for platform operations or by law. Personal data is not intended for public display or unrelated marketing.

Security and retention

Technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect account information, but no electronic system can guarantee absolute security. Records may be retained as needed for partner account operation, bookings, payments, accounting, security, dispute handling and legal requirements. Different records may have different retention periods.

Your choices and contact

Use Partner account settings to update information where the feature is available. For privacy questions or account-data requests, use the configured support options on the Partner Help page. Ownership verification may be required.

Effective date: 25 November 2026.
