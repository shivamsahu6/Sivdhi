About Sivdhi Partner

Sivdhi Partner provides a place to coordinate the workers linked to a Partner account, view job information and manage Partner account features.

Partner Account

Use your registered Partner account to sign in. Keep your profile and bank information accurate, and protect your password and OTP.

Worker Coordination

The Partner area provides access to the workers linked to your account and the available worker management and availability controls.

Job Management

Review job information assigned to your Partner account and use the available actions to accept, reject or update job progress where those actions are offered. Every assigned Worker must show their Sivdhi Worker ID Card to the customer on arrival at each in-person service visit, before starting work. Customers can view the assigned card in My Bookings; no customer verification button is required.

Partner Wallet

The Partner Wallet displays the balance and payment information currently available in your account. Withdrawal requests follow the controls and approval process displayed in the Partner area.

Partner Responsibilities

Maintain accurate account information, coordinate the workers linked to your account, ensure every Worker shows their Sivdhi Worker ID Card to the customer at every in-person service visit, use the available job controls responsibly and keep account credentials and OTPs private. The Sivdhi Worker ID Card is a platform-issued card, not a government identity document or proof of government/police verification.
