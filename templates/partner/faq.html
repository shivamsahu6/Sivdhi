❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

How do I withdraw my earnings?
Open Wallet → Withdraw Money → Enter amount → Submit request. After admin approval, the amount will be transferred to your registered bank account.
Why can't I withdraw money?
Please make sure your bank details are added and your wallet balance is sufficient.
How can I change my bank account?
Go to Profile → Bank Details. Update your details and verify them using the OTP sent to your registered email.
How can I contact support?
Open Help & Support and contact us via Call, WhatsApp or Email.
How do I edit my profile?
Open your Profile, tap the 3-dot menu, then select Edit Profile.
