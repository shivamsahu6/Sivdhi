About Sivdhi Worker

Sivdhi Worker provides a place to view assigned job offers, manage work updates and access worker account information.

Worker Account

Use your registered Worker account to sign in and access the Worker pages. Keep your account and profile information accurate, and protect your password and OTP.

Job Offers

Review the job details and available actions presented in your Worker account. Offers can be accepted or rejected through the provided controls.

Work Updates

Use the available job actions to record arrival, start the work and mark the job complete when the work has been completed. At every in-person service visit, you must show your Sivdhi Worker ID Card to the customer on arrival, before starting work. You can show it on your phone or as a printed card. Keep your photo, name and Worker ID visible. Customers can view the assigned card in My Bookings; they do not need to submit a verification button.

Wallet

The Worker Wallet shows the earnings information and payment records currently available in your account. Any withdrawal or payment action is subject to the controls shown in the account.

Worker Responsibilities

Keep your profile and location information accurate, review each offer before responding, follow the job workflow, show your Sivdhi Worker ID Card to the customer at every in-person visit, and keep account credentials and OTPs private. The Sivdhi Worker ID Card is a platform-issued card, not a government identity document.

Help & Support

Read the Worker FAQs or visit Help & Support for the configured support options.
