🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Information we collect

Sivdhi may process information you provide during registration and account use, including your name, mobile number, email, profile photo, address, skills, experience, bank details, job history, location and account activity. The information used depends on the features you access.

How information is used

Information is used to create and maintain your Worker account, verify details, match and manage job offers, record arrival and completion, maintain wallet and settlement records, send relevant notifications and respond to support requests. Your profile photo, name, public Worker ID and service skills are also used to generate your Sivdhi Worker ID Card and display the assigned Worker identity to the relevant customer in My Bookings for a booking. The card is platform-issued and is not a government identity document or proof of government/police verification.

Location and job information

Location information may be used to determine service coverage, distance and eligibility for job offers. Job details are available to authorised roles only as needed to coordinate and complete the booking.

Bank and payment information

Bank details and transaction references are used for verification, eligible payout processing and reconciliation. Do not send passwords, OTPs, PINs or full payment credentials through ordinary support messages.

Information sharing

Information may be made available to authorised Sivdhi personnel, a partner coordinating the work, payment or messaging providers, hosting providers, or a lawful authority where needed for platform operations or required by law. Information is not intended for unrelated marketing or public display.

Security and retention

Technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect information. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. Records may be retained as needed for account operation, job history, payments, accounting, security, disputes and legal requirements. A single fixed retention period does not apply to every record.

Your choices and contact

Review the profile and settings available in your Worker account to update permitted information. For a privacy question or account-data request, use the configured support options on the Worker Help page. Ownership verification may be required.

Effective date: 28 November 2026.
