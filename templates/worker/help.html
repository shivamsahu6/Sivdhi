Help

Guides and answers for your Sivdhi Worker account

How can we help?

Find guidance for job offers, booking progress, availability, wallet records and your account.

📋 How do I view available jobs? ⌄
Open your Worker Home or Jobs section to view current job offers and assigned bookings. Review the service, location and requested schedule before accepting an offer.
✅ How do I accept or reject a job? ⌄
Open the job details and check that you can complete the service at the listed time and location. Use the Accept or Reject action shown for that offer before its response window expires.
🔄 How do I update a booking's progress? ⌄
Open the assigned job and use the available job actions in order, such as Arrived, Start and Complete. Only update a status when that step has actually happened.
💰 Where can I check my wallet and earnings? ⌄
Open the Wallet section to review the entries recorded for your account. Earnings and settlement details depend on eligible completed bookings and payments recorded by Sivdhi.
🔐 How do I reset a forgotten password? ⌄
Open the Worker Login page and choose Forgot Password. Enter the email registered to your account, verify the six-digit OTP sent to that email, and then set your new password. Check your spam folder if the OTP does not arrive. Never share the OTP with anyone.
📍 How do I update my profile or service location? ⌄
Open your Worker Profile and choose Edit Profile to review the information and service location saved for your account. Keep your work area and availability details accurate.

Need more help?

For questions about jobs, booking updates, wallet records or your Worker account, contact the Sivdhi support team or browse the Worker FAQs.
