❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

How can I check my earnings?
Open Wallet to view your total earnings, pending payments and payment history.
How can I withdraw my earnings?
After complete job admin dopsit your earnings in your account
Why can't I withdraw money?
Please make sure your bank details are added correctly and your wallet has sufficient balance. If the problem continues, contact Help & Support.
How can I change my bank account?
Go to Profile → Bank Details. Update your bank details and verify the change using the OTP sent to your registered email.
How can I edit my profile?
Open your Profile and select Edit Profile. You can update your personal information, address, skills, experience and profile photo.
How can I change my password?
Go to Settings → Change Password. Enter your current password and your new password. An OTP will be sent to your registered email for verification.
Where can I see my jobs?
Open My Jobs from the worker dashboard to view your available and assigned jobs.
How can I contact support?
Open Help & Support and contact the Sivdhi support team through the available support options.
