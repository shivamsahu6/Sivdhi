Acceptance and scope

By using a Sivdhi Worker account, you agree to these Worker Terms and the Worker Privacy Policy. These terms apply to worker account access, job offers, assigned work, wallet information and support.

Registration and account information

Provide accurate identity, contact, profile, skill and experience information. Keep your registered contact details current and complete verification steps requested through the platform. Do not create duplicate or misleading accounts or share your login credentials.

Job offers and assignments

Review the service, location, schedule and instructions shown with each offer. Accept work only when you are available and able to perform it. A live offer may temporarily reserve your availability. After accepting a job, keep its status updated through the Worker portal and do not accept conflicting active work.

Arrival, work and completion

Record arrival through the platform at the service location. At every in-person service visit, you are required to show your official Sivdhi Worker ID Card to the customer when you arrive, before starting the work. You may display the card on your phone or present a printed copy. Keep your card accessible, make sure your photo, name and Worker ID are visible, and never allow another person to use your card. The customer may view the assigned Worker ID Card in My Bookings; the customer is not required to press a verification or confirmation button. The Sivdhi Worker ID Card is a platform-issued identification card only. It is not a government-issued identity document and does not, by itself, certify government or police verification. Start and complete work using the available job actions. A job may be completed in Sivdhi only after the required arrival and payment conditions have been recorded. Never mark a job completed when the service has not actually been completed.

Professional conduct and customer privacy

Perform accepted work carefully and respectfully. Use customer information only for the assigned service. Do not disclose customer phone numbers, address or booking details outside the service purpose, use them for marketing or publish them.

Earnings, wallet and withdrawals

Eligible earnings are recorded through Sivdhi's booking settlement process after completion. Wallet amounts and withdrawal status depend on the settlement record and payment mode. Demo or sandbox payment records do not represent real funds. Withdrawal requests are subject to the status and verification steps shown in the Worker portal and Admin review where required.

Bank details and account security

Enter accurate bank details and complete the verification steps shown by Sivdhi. Protect your password and OTP. Sivdhi support will not ask you to disclose your password or OTP.

Safety, complaints and suspension

Report unsafe conditions, booking problems, payment issues or suspected misuse through Help & Support. Sivdhi may restrict or suspend accounts for fraud, false information, unsafe conduct, privacy violations or material misuse, subject to applicable law.

Privacy, updates and contact

Read the Worker Privacy Policy for information about profile, job, location, bank and account data. These terms may be updated when platform features or applicable requirements change. For help, use the configured support options in the Worker Help page.
