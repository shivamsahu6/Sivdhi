What does a Sivdhi Partner do?

A Partner helps coordinate home-service activity for workers linked to their Partner account. The work is managed through the Partner area of Sivdhi.

  • View and manage workers linked to the Partner account.
  • Coordinate service bookings and respond to assigned job activity.
  • Follow booking progress and receive service notifications.
  • Review the Partner wallet and submit a bank withdrawal request when eligible balance is available.

The role can be managed using a mobile phone or laptop. Partners should be able to communicate with their linked workers and coordinate activity in the areas where Sivdhi is building service coverage.

Work from home and flexible-time opportunity

Sivdhi is building its worker and service network gradually. People from different parts of India can register as Partners, even while customer service availability is still developing in their own area.

Partner coordination can be handled remotely through a phone or laptop. The time needed may depend on the number of linked workers, the bookings received, and the activity in the Partner's area.

Important: Partner registration being open across India does not mean home services are already available in every city. Customer bookings depend on local worker availability and service coverage.

How Partner commission works

The Sivdhi Partner role is commission-based, not a fixed monthly-salary job. A Partner commission is linked to eligible bookings completed by a worker assigned to that Partner.

  1. The Partner's commission percentage is configured for the service and saved with the booking.
  2. The booking must be marked completed, and both the advance and remaining customer payment must be recorded as paid.
  3. Sivdhi then credits the Partner commission to the Partner wallet. The settlement is recorded once for that booking.
  4. The Partner can submit a withdrawal request to the bank account saved in the Partner profile. The transfer is subject to Sivdhi Admin approval.

The code's default commission configuration is 10% for the Partner and 10% for Sivdhi, with the remaining amount allocated to the Worker. Service-specific settings can change these percentages, so 10% is not a guaranteed rate for every service or booking.

No guaranteed earnings: There is no fixed salary or guaranteed monthly income. Commission depends on linked workers, eligible bookings, completed services, full customer payment, and the commission rate configured for that service.

Who can register?

People interested in coordinating local home-service workers and bookings can create a Sivdhi Partner account. A phone or laptop with internet access is needed to use the online Partner area.

Partner registration is separate from Worker registration. Workers provide services at customer locations; Partners coordinate workers and booking activity.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sivdhi Partner work from home?

Partner account activities can be managed remotely using a mobile phone or laptop. Coordination relates to workers and service bookings in the Partner's local operating area.

Is this a part-time job with a fixed salary?

No. This is a commission-based Partner opportunity, not a salaried employment vacancy. The code does not promise a fixed salary or guaranteed income.

How is commission credited?

After an eligible booking is completed and the advance and remaining customer payment are both recorded as paid, Sivdhi credits the service-wise Partner commission to the Partner wallet.

Can I withdraw my Partner wallet balance?

A Partner can submit a withdrawal request to the saved bank account. Sivdhi Admin reviews and approves the request before the bank transfer.

Can I register if Sivdhi has no workers in my city yet?

Partner registration can be created from across India. Actual customer bookings in an area depend on local worker availability and service coverage, which Sivdhi is building gradually.

Is Partner registration the same as Worker registration?

No. Workers perform the home services; Partners manage linked workers and coordinate service booking activity.
