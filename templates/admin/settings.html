Internal Commission Split

These percentages are used by Sivdhi's internal settlement after service completion. Customer price and checkout do not show a separate Platform Fee.

Partner receives this percentage when assigned worker belongs to a registered Partner.

This is an internal revenue share, not an extra amount charged on top to the customer.

Partner + Sivdhi total cannot exceed 100%. Worker receives the remaining share. If the worker has no Partner, the Partner share is 0% and worker receives the remaining amount after Sivdhi's configured share. New settings apply to future bookings; completed bookings are not recalculated.
