Our Story · India

What is Sivdhi?

Sivdhi is an India-based online home-services discovery and booking platform. It helps customers explore listed services, check availability for their location, review displayed prices and request a service booking through one website.

Who founded Sivdhi?

Sivdhi was founded by Shivam Sahu, who envisioned a simpler digital way for people to discover and request everyday home services. The platform brings the customer booking journey and service-professional workflow together online.

Why was Sivdhi created?

Finding a suitable person for a household repair or service can take time and involve uncertainty. Sivdhi was created with the aim of making service discovery and booking more organised: customers can view listed options, check local availability, see the price shown before proceeding and follow booking updates in one place. The platform also provides a digital workflow for service professionals to receive and manage eligible job requests.

When did Sivdhi launch?

Sivdhi’s public website launch date is 21 November 2026. The platform continues to evolve as its service catalogue, booking experience and professional workflows are improved.

What services does Sivdhi offer?

Sivdhi lists home-service categories and subcategories, including household repair and maintenance options where available. The live catalogue on the website is the source of current services and prices. Service availability depends on the selected service, location and available professionals; Sivdhi does not claim that every service is available in every city.

How does Sivdhi work?

Customers visit the Sivdhi website, choose Normal or Full-Day service where offered, select a listed category and service, provide the requested location and booking details, review the amount and complete the payment steps shown at checkout. The platform then processes the booking and worker-matching workflow. Customers can follow available updates in their account under Bookings.

Payments, privacy and service information

Customers should review the final booking details and payment status on the website. Payment confirmation is handled through the configured payment flow; never share an OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN or password with anyone. Account and booking information is handled under the Privacy Policy. Platform availability, service scope and displayed prices can vary by location and catalogue updates.

Help, FAQs and customer support

For common questions, visit Sivdhi FAQs or Help. Customers can use the Contact Us support facility after signing in. Review the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy before booking.
