About Us

About Sivdhi

Sivdhi Customer is a service platform where customers can explore listed home services, review prices and manage their booking journey in one place.

सेवाएँ

Browse active service categories and their subcategories from the Customer Homepage. Service prices shown on the platform are taken from the configured catalogue. Availability depends on the selected service type and the service area checked for your location.

Account Creation

You can explore the public service catalogue without signing in. An account is required when you choose a subcategory and continue towards booking. Keep the information you submit for your account accurate and current.

डेटा इस्तेमाल करने की सहमति

Information submitted while using the customer website is handled according to the Privacy Policy. Please review that policy before submitting account, location or booking information.

बुकिंग

Choose the Normal or Full-Day service type, open a category, select a subcategory and follow the booking steps shown on the website. Before a booking proceeds, Sivdhi checks whether the selected service is available in the chosen area.

आपकी जिम्मेदारियाँ

Provide correct account and booking details, choose the intended service and service address, review the displayed price and booking information, and keep your sign-in credentials and OTP private.

शिकायत निवारण

For common questions, visit Help or FAQs. To create or track a customer support request, use the existing Contact Us facility after signing in.
