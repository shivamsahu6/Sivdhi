About this privacy notice

This notice describes information used by current Sivdhi website features, the purposes for which it is handled and the technology providers used to operate those features. The information involved depends on the role and services you choose to use.

01 Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Sivdhi may process information that you submit while registering, maintaining your account or requesting a service.

  • Profile information: information such as your name, email address and other details you choose to provide.
  • Booking information: selected services, service address, booking status, related transaction references and, after assignment, the Worker's profile photo, name, public Worker ID and service skills shown for that booking.
  • Support messages: information you provide when reporting an issue or requesting assistance.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser or log information where the website or its infrastructure records it.

The exact information collected depends on the features implemented and the permissions you grant.

02 How information is used

Information may be used for legitimate platform operations, including:

  • Creating and managing your customer account.
  • Processing bookings and displaying their status.
  • Finding available service providers for a booking.
  • Showing the assigned Worker's platform-issued ID Card in My Bookings so you can compare the arriving person with the assigned Worker details.
  • Sending relevant service and account notifications.
  • Responding to support requests and resolving disputes.
  • Protecting accounts and identifying suspicious activity.
  • Maintaining and improving website functionality.

The Worker ID Card is a Sivdhi platform-issued card, not a government-issued identity document or proof of government or police verification. Customers can view the assigned card in My Bookings; no in-app verification button is required. The card is displayed for booking-related identification and safety purposes only.

Any additional use should be explained to customers and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

03 Location information

If you enter a service address or choose to share your location, that information may be used to determine service availability and match a booking with an eligible provider.

Browser-based location access generally requires permission. You can manage location permissions through your browser or device settings.

Sivdhi uses the location information needed to check service coverage and coordinate a requested booking. Browser location access requires your permission; you can also manage location access in your browser or device settings.

04 When information may be shared

Information may need to be processed by systems or people involved in providing the service, subject to the platform's actual workflow and applicable safeguards.

  • Service fulfilment: limited booking details may be needed to arrange the requested service.
  • Technology providers: hosting, messaging or other providers may process information if the project uses their services.
  • Legal or safety requirements: information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Customer and worker personal phone numbers should not be exposed on pages where they are not required. Access to personal information should be limited to authorized purposes.

05Public customer reviews

After a booking is marked completed, the signed-in customer can submit one star rating and an optional written review for that booking.

Reviews appear on the public Sivdhi Reviews page with the service name, rating, review text and submission date. The public page does not display the customer's name, phone number, address or booking reference.

Do not include personal, financial or sensitive details in review text. Reviews may be hidden or removed where necessary for safety, legal compliance or platform integrity.

06 Analytics and cookies

Sivdhi has an optional Google Analytics 4 (GA4) integration. As of 24 November 2026, GA4 collection is not active on Sivdhi Production because its Measurement ID has not been configured there. If enabled later, the tag is restricted to approved public entry pages: the customer home and signup pages, the public partner opportunity page, and the public worker/partner login and signup pages. It is excluded from signed-in sessions, bookings, profiles, dashboards and the Admin Panel.

When active, the GA4 stream is configured for page-view and scroll measurement. Sivdhi sends a page location containing only the website origin and path, without the query string, and reduces the referrer to its origin. Google Signals and ad-personalisation signals are disabled in the tag configuration.

The GA4 stream has Email Redaction and selected URL query-parameter redaction configured. These are best-effort safeguards, not a guarantee that every personal value will be detected or removed. URL redaction applies only to selected keys in specified event parameters. Do not put personal, account, booking or payment information in URLs or page titles.

Sivdhi also uses session and security mechanisms needed for website and account functions. If GA4 is enabled, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure public-page use, subject to Google settings and the visitor's browser configuration.

07 Data security

Sivdhi uses technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Safeguards may include access controls, secure password handling, protected connections, server-side validation and appropriate database permissions.

No website or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Never send passwords, OTPs, PINs or full payment credentials through ordinary support messages.

08 How long information is retained

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, accounting, security and dispute resolution requirements.

Sivdhi does not currently publish one fixed retention period for every type of record. Different records may need to be kept for different periods to operate accounts and bookings, reconcile payments, handle support requests and disputes, protect the platform, and meet applicable accounting or legal requirements.

09 Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights or options regarding your personal information, including requesting access to or correction of certain details, changing notification preferences or seeking deletion where permitted.

You can review your profile and the available settings through your account. Some information may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate operational and dispute-resolution purposes.

To ask a question or make a request, use the Help & Support page.

10 Children's privacy

Do not submit a child's personal information through Sivdhi unless you are the child's parent or lawful guardian, or otherwise legally authorised to do so. If you believe a child's information has been submitted inappropriately, contact Sivdhi using the details below.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted inappropriately, contact support so the matter can be reviewed.

11 Changes to this policy

This policy may need updating when website features, information practices or applicable legal requirements change.
