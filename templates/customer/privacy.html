This page explains the types of information that may be used when you access Sivdhi, place bookings or manage your account.

01 Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Sivdhi may process information that you submit while registering, maintaining your account or requesting a service.

  • Profile information: information such as your name, email address and other details you choose to provide.
  • Booking information: selected services, service address, booking status and related transaction references.
  • Support messages: information you provide when reporting an issue or requesting assistance.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser or log information where the website or its infrastructure records it.

The exact information collected depends on the features implemented and the permissions you grant.

02 How information is used

Information may be used for legitimate platform operations, including:

  • Creating and managing your customer account.
  • Processing bookings and displaying their status.
  • Finding available service providers for a booking.
  • Sending relevant service and account notifications.
  • Responding to support requests and resolving disputes.
  • Protecting accounts and identifying suspicious activity.
  • Maintaining and improving website functionality.

Any additional use should be explained to customers and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

03 Location information

If you enter a service address or choose to share your location, that information may be used to determine service availability and match a booking with an eligible provider.

Browser-based location access generally requires permission. You can manage location permissions through your browser or device settings.

The project should collect and display only the location details necessary for the service and should explain any additional use clearly.

04 When information may be shared

Information may need to be processed by systems or people involved in providing the service, subject to the platform's actual workflow and applicable safeguards.

  • Service fulfilment: limited booking details may be needed to arrange the requested service.
  • Technology providers: hosting, messaging or other providers may process information if the project uses their services.
  • Legal or safety requirements: information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Customer and worker personal phone numbers should not be exposed on pages where they are not required. Access to personal information should be limited to authorized purposes.

05 Data security

Sivdhi should use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Safeguards may include access controls, secure password handling, protected connections, server-side validation and appropriate database permissions.

No website or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Never send passwords, OTPs, PINs or full payment credentials through ordinary support messages.

06 How long information is retained

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, accounting, security and dispute resolution requirements.

The specific retention periods should be established according to the real database, operational needs and legal obligations of the service.

07 Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights or options regarding your personal information, including requesting access to or correction of certain details, changing notification preferences or seeking deletion where permitted.

You can review your profile and the available settings through your account. Some information may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate operational and dispute-resolution purposes.

To ask a question or make a request, use the Help & Support page.

08 Children's privacy

Sivdhi should define appropriate eligibility requirements for its services and avoid collecting personal information from children where doing so is not permitted.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted inappropriately, contact support so the matter can be reviewed.

09 Changes to this policy

This policy may need updating when website features, information practices or applicable legal requirements change.

Any published version should indicate its effective or last-updated date and explain material changes where appropriate.

10 Contact and questions

For privacy-related questions, requests or concerns, use the available customer support channel.

Do not include your password, OTP, PIN or full bank account number when contacting support.
