📋 How do I book a service? +

Open Home, choose a service category and subcategory, review the displayed price, enter your service location and select the required quantity.

Review the booking details and follow the available checkout steps to submit your booking.

🗓️ Where can I see my booking status? +

Open the Bookings tab to review your bookings and their available status information.

Status updates depend on the booking information recorded by the Sivdhi system.

👷 What if no worker has been assigned? +

Worker assignment depends on availability and the matching rules configured for the service.

Check your booking status again later. If the issue continues, contact support and include your booking reference.

🛡️ What worker information will I see? +

Your booking page should display the assignment status and the permitted worker information available for your booking.

Personal phone numbers should remain private and should not be exposed on the customer booking page.

₹ How is the booking amount calculated? +

The displayed amount should be based on the configured service price and the selected quantity.

Review the final amount on the booking or checkout page before submitting your booking.

💳 What should I do if a payment fails? +

Check your booking status and your payment confirmation before trying again. Avoid repeating a payment while its status is uncertain.

If an amount was deducted but the payment status remains unclear, contact support with the booking reference and payment information. Never send an OTP, PIN or password.

↩ How can I cancel a booking? +

Open Bookings and select the relevant booking. Use the cancellation option if it is available for its current status.

Cancellation eligibility and any refund depend on the rules implemented for the service and payment. Contact support if no cancellation option is shown.

👤 How do I update my profile? +

Open Profile and select Edit Profile. Update the permitted information and save your changes.

Some account fields may require additional verification, depending on the system rules.

🔔 How do I change notification settings? +

Open Profile, go to Settings and review the available notification preferences.

Notification delivery depends on the permissions and notification services configured for the site.

💬 How do I contact Sivdhi support? +

Open Help & Support from your Profile page and submit a description of your issue.

Include the booking reference when it is relevant, but do not include your password, OTP, PIN or complete bank account details.
