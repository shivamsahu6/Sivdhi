01Acceptance and scope

By creating a Customer account or using Customer features on Sivdhi, you agree to these Terms & Conditions and the Privacy Policy. These terms apply to customers browsing services, requesting bookings, making payments, contacting support and reviewing completed services.

Sivdhi provides the digital platform and booking workflow. A booking request or payment receipt does not by itself prove that a worker has been assigned or that the service has been completed. Your account displays the latest booking status recorded by the system.

02Customer account and security

Provide accurate name, mobile number and email details when registering. Keep your contact information current. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account.

  • Keep your password and OTP private.
  • Use your own account and do not impersonate another person.
  • Report suspected unauthorised access through Help & Support.
  • Account access may be restricted in cases of suspected fraud, misuse or security concerns, subject to applicable law.

03Services, prices and area coverage

The Customer catalogue displays active service categories, subcategories and configured customer prices. Viewing a category or price does not reserve a worker or guarantee service coverage.

When you select a subcategory, you must sign in before choosing or confirming the service location. Sivdhi checks whether the selected service can be served in that area before allowing the booking flow to continue.

04Bookings, schedule and worker assignment

Review the selected service, address, date, time, quantity and displayed amount before submitting a booking. The booking page displays the latest status recorded by Sivdhi, such as Finding a Worker, Booking Confirmed, Worker Arrived, Booking Started or Booking Completed.

Worker assignment depends on service coverage, worker eligibility and availability. A pending or unassigned booking is not a confirmed worker assignment. Do not assume a worker has been assigned unless the booking status shows Booking Confirmed.

Keep the service address accessible and booking details accurate. Contact Support promptly if displayed details appear incorrect.

05Prices, advance and balance payments

Customer prices are shown in the catalogue and booking flow. The booking total is calculated from the configured service price, selected quantity and any other amounts expressly shown before submission. Internal partner or platform commission settings are not a separate customer charge unless displayed as part of your booking total.

Where the booking flow requests an advance payment, the amount and payment status are shown during checkout. Any remaining balance is displayed in your booking and becomes payable after the assigned worker records arrival. Use only payment methods presented in the Sivdhi booking flow.

A payment is successful only when its status is confirmed by the payment provider and recorded by Sivdhi. Demo or sandbox receipts are test records and are not proof that real money was collected. Do not repeat a payment while its status is uncertain; check the booking status or contact Support.

06Cancellation and refunds

The Cancel option is available only while a booking is eligible for customer cancellation. The self-service option is generally limited to bookings that have not yet been assigned to a worker. Once a worker is assigned, use Help & Support to report a cancellation request or issue; submitting a request does not itself cancel the booking.

Refund eligibility and amount depend on the booking state, payments actually received and the applicable payment-provider process. A refund shown as pending or queued is not complete until the payment provider confirms it. Some cases may need Admin review or reconciliation. No refund timeline is promised unless it is displayed in the applicable payment process.

Keep your booking and payment references when contacting Support. Never send an OTP, password, PIN or full payment credentials in a support message.

07Service completion and complaints

A booking is treated as completed only after the worker records the required job completion steps and Sivdhi updates the booking status. A payment being recorded does not alone mean the service has been completed.

If the worker did not arrive, work was incomplete, service quality was an issue or the booking status appears incorrect, raise a private Support Request with the booking reference and a clear description.

08Customer reviews and public display

After a booking is marked completed, you may submit one star rating and an optional written review for that booking. The review form appears directly below the completed booking in My Bookings.

Submitted reviews appear on Sivdhi's public Reviews page. The page displays the service name, rating, review text and date; it does not display your name, phone number, address or booking reference. Do not include personal, financial or sensitive information in a review.

Reviews must relate to your actual service experience and must not contain unlawful, threatening, misleading, abusive or private information. Sivdhi may hide or remove content where necessary for safety, legal compliance or platform integrity.

09Customer responsibilities and fair use

  • Provide a valid service address and accurate booking information.
  • Be available at the agreed location and time and provide safe, reasonable access for the requested work.
  • Treat workers and Sivdhi support personnel respectfully.
  • Do not misuse the platform, submit false claims or access another person's account or booking.
  • Do not use Sivdhi for unlawful activities or request work outside the selected service scope.

10Privacy and security

Sivdhi processes account, location, booking, payment-reference and support information to operate the website and requested services. Access to information is limited to purposes needed to operate, secure and support the platform and comply with applicable requirements.

Personal phone numbers and private account details are not displayed on the Customer booking page. Read the Privacy Policy for details about data handling, public reviews and your choices.

11Platform availability and updates

Sivdhi may update service listings, booking features or website systems. Temporary maintenance, technical faults, network issues or payment-provider interruptions may affect access. Booking and payment outcomes are based on the latest status recorded by the system and payment provider.

Changes to these terms will be published on this page with an updated date. Nothing in these terms removes a right that cannot legally be excluded.

12Support and disputes

For booking, service, payment, cancellation, privacy or account questions, open Help & Support. To create a private Support Request or view replies, sign in to your Customer account and open Support Requests.

Where an issue cannot be resolved through Support, you may use consumer or other legal remedies available under applicable law. Nothing in these terms limits statutory or other rights that cannot lawfully be waived.
