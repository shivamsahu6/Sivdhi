Help

Guides and answers for your Sivdhi customer account

How can we help?

Find guidance for bookings, payments, your account and service reviews.

📋 How do I book a service? ⌄
Browse categories and prices without signing in. Select a subcategory; sign in first, then choose your service location and check whether the selected service is available in your area.
🔎 How can I check my booking status? ⌄
Open the Bookings section to view your bookings and their currently available status information.
👷 What if a worker has not been assigned? ⌄
A booking may show Finding a Worker while Sivdhi checks worker eligibility and availability. Assignment is confirmed only when your booking status changes to Booking Confirmed.
💳 Where can I check my booking amount? ⌄
Review the configured service price and booking total before confirming. Where an advance is collected, any remaining balance is shown in your booking and becomes due after the worker arrives.
🔐 How do I change my password? ⌄
Open Profile, choose Settings, then Change Password. Complete the verification steps displayed by Sivdhi.
👤 How do I update my profile? ⌄
Open your Profile and select Edit Profile to view the profile information that can be updated.

Need more help?

Browse the detailed Customer FAQs or sign in to create a private Support Request and follow replies. Booking and account information stays inside your signed-in account.
