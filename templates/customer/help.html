Help with your Sivdhi customer account

How can we help?

Find answers to common questions about bookings and your account.

📋 How do I book a service? ⌄
Open Home, select a service category and subcategory, review the price, enter your location and choose the required quantity. Continue through the booking flow to submit your request.
🔎 How can I check my booking status? ⌄
Open the Bookings section to view your bookings and their currently available status information.
👷 What if a worker has not been assigned? ⌄
The booking may still be awaiting worker assignment. Check the booking status again for the latest information.
💳 Where can I check my booking amount? ⌄
Review the service price and total shown during the booking process. Refer to your booking details for the amount associated with your request.
🔐 How do I change my password? ⌄
Open your Profile, go to Settings and select Change Password. Follow the verification instructions shown on the screen.
👤 How do I update my profile? ⌄
Open your Profile and select Edit Profile to view the profile information that can be updated.
